Sources and how each defines its limits

Each column is one source, and limits are shown exactly as that source defines them. A 10th–90th percentile range, an interquartile range and a laboratory reference interval are not interchangeable, so pick the limit and source that fit your laboratory's method.

The first column, All sources, runs from the lowest lower limit to the highest upper limit among the sources shown; it is a quick overview, not a reference interval. The bar in each cell draws that source's range on a scale shared by the row, with a tick at the median. A marker is tagged "sources differ" when upper limits differ twofold or more, or lower limits threefold or more, leaving interquartile ranges out of that comparison. Group sizes under 20 are flagged.

Counts were published as cells/µL, ×10³/µL or ×10⁹/L and are converted to the unit you choose. Age groups printed as closed ranges, such as 7–8 years, run up to the start of the next group. Preterm infants, cord blood and Saudi or Gulf cohorts are not covered.

For reference only. The reporting laboratory's own validated intervals take precedence in clinical interpretation.
